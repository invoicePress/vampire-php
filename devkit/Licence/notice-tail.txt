   Publisher: Koordinierungsstelle fuer IT-Standards (KoSIT)

D. Test material, only in the development tree, never in a runtime export
   XRechnung test suite 3.0.2 (KoSIT, Apache-2.0) and the test files of

sets above, as required to stay comparable with the reference validator.
